## Belief vs measurement

Beliefs: OpenAI (AGI era), NVIDIA (AGI arrived), Anthropic CEO (1-3 years),
DeepMind (2029-2030), SSI (5-20 years). The spread runs from "already" to "two
decades".

Measurements from the same organisations: no sustained AI-attributable 2x
acceleration (Anthropic, Sept 2026); below the "High" self-improvement threshold
(OpenAI, Sept 2026); "likely unable to fully and reliably automate R&D for frontier
projects spanning multiple weeks" (METR on Mythos 5, June 2026).

The model follows the measurements and treats the beliefs as comparison only.
